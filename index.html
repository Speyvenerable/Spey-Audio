Paste a direct audio link (.mp3 .m4a .ogg .wav .flac...). Files are stored inside this browser and play with no internet. Cover art and lyrics inside the file are picked up automatically. Tap a song to open its music box with the cover and lyrics. Links from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud and similar platforms can't be downloaded by a browser, so they're kept as online-only bookmarks. You can also drop audio files onto this panel.

SETTINGS

Some servers block downloads from other sites (CORS). If you run your own relay, e.g. a small Cloudflare Worker, put its address here. Use {url} where the audio link goes, or the link gets added at the end. Only use a relay you control.

This sends the song title and artist to lrclib.net (lyrics) and Apple's iTunes search (cover art). Nothing else is sent. Turn it off to keep everything fully local; you can still press FIND LYRICS in the music box any time.

  1. Open a site above, pick a track and use its Download button to save the .mp3 on your device.
  2. Come back here and press LOAD FILES. This is the most reliable way, and the cover art and lyrics inside the file come with it.
  3. To save by URL instead: long-press (or right-click) the download button, choose Copy link address, and paste it into AUDIO URL. The link must point straight to the audio file.

If a site refuses the URL save (CORS), use LOAD FILES. Only keep music you have the right to use. Streaming links (YouTube, Spotify...) can't be turned into audio files here.
